FixSaathi stores short paraphrased facts (symptom, listed causes, checks, the manufacturer's advice) with a link back. It does not copy manual text. Possible causes are stored as the source lists them, not as confirmed diagnoses.

**Evidence levels**

- *manufacturer documented*: the manufacturer's own support page. Status `verified_source`.
- *manufacturer general guidance*: a guide the manufacturer published on its own site, not tied to one model. Status `verified_source`.
- *manufacturer manual, secondary host*: the manufacturer's manual, hosted by a retailer or manual archive. We could not confirm the copy, so the status is `unverified`.

**Other sources**

- ApplianceDB free developer sample, 280 records, ODbL-1.0. "Contains data from ApplianceDB (https://github.com/ApplianceDB/ApplianceDB-public), licensed under ODbL v1.0." Each record keeps its upstream `source_url`.
