## 2. "Who pays?" answer (use as a slide + spoken answer)

- **Customer:** appliance service companies and repair platforms (they own the callbacks and safety risk).
- **Pays for:** a per-technician monthly seat. Value: fewer repeat visits, fewer accidents, faster onboarding of new technicians, a report for every job.
- **Cost to serve:** our dashboard measures AI cost per repair, so we can show the margin with real numbers (use the live figure from the dashboard).
- **Say:** "We start with one service company, measure first-time-fix rate and cost per repair, then price per technician per month."
- Do NOT quote market-size numbers unless you can name the source (ICRA, CEEW, MNRE sources are in the deck).
